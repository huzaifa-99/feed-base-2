Privacy Policy for FeedBase2

Last updated: September 19, 2026
App / site: https://feedbase2.rhuzaifa.com/
Contact: dev@rhuzaifa.com

FeedBase2 (“the App”) is a binary practice game available on Android and on the web. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App or visit feedbase2.rhuzaifa.com, and how it is used.

Consent

By using FeedBase2, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

FeedBase2 does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number to play.

We may process:

  • Gameplay preferences stored on your device — for example high scores, selected mode/difficulty, sound, controls, and appearance settings (via browser or app local storage).
  • Usage analytics — standard aggregated analytics (such as Google Analytics) which may include approximate location derived from IP address, device/browser type, pages or screens viewed, and similar technical data. This is used to understand how the App is used and to improve it.
  • Server / hosting logs — typical web hosting logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps, requested URLs) used for security, reliability, and debugging.
  • Messages you send us — if you email support, we receive whatever you include in that message.

How we use information

  • Provide and operate FeedBase2
  • Remember your settings and scores on your device
  • Understand usage trends and improve features
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Respond if you contact us

Local storage and cookies

The App stores preferences and scores locally on your device so gameplay works between sessions (including offline after the first load). Analytics providers may also use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies.

Third-party services

We use Google services for analytics (and, where applicable, related measurement tools). Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more about ads-related cookies (if shown in your region/tools) at Google’s advertising technologies page.

We do not sell your personal information.

Children

FeedBase2 is a general-audience educational game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through contact email, please contact us and we will delete it where appropriate.

Data retention

Local settings remain on your device until you clear app/site storage. Analytics and hosting logs are retained according to our providers’ and host’s retention settings, typically for a limited period needed for analysis and security.

Your choices

  • Clear the App’s storage or site data in your device/browser settings to remove local scores and preferences.
  • Use platform/browser controls to limit analytics or cookies where available.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.

California / GDPR-style requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data we hold (for example, information in an email you sent us). Contact dev@rhuzaifa.com and we will respond within a reasonable time (typically within one month where required by law).

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a new version at this URL. Changes apply when posted. The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly.
